# 暮色滑翔

A landscape 3D glide game over a sunset archipelago, rendered with three.js. A paper bird slides along the hills of one island after another while the sun goes down; the round ends when night falls and the bird settles. Every model, texture and sound is generated by code; the pack loads no image, model, font or audio files.

## Play

- One control. Touch and hold anywhere (or hold Space / ↓ / S / Enter, or the mouse button) to make the bird heavy; release to let it glide. P / Esc pauses.
- Heavy on a downslope gains speed; heavy on an upslope loses it. Releasing on the way up lets the hill throw the bird into the air when its curve falls away faster than gravity can hold the bird down.
- Landing along a downslope (flight path within 0.25 rad of the slope) is a perfect landing: a speed boost and points that grow with the chain. Three in a row start 顺风, which doubles all points until a clumsy landing or a splash. A landing into an upslope costs speed and the chain; a fast, shallow touch on the sea skips like a stone.
- 余晖 (daylight) starts at 56 seconds. Reaching each new island adds daylight (22 seconds, falling to 9 for later islands) and arrival points; reaching one after nightfall wakes the bird again. Flights over 0.6 s add airtime points, and reaching 24 m above the ground touches the clouds once per flight. Score never decreases.

## Files

- `rules.js` — the deterministic simulation (`XiabanDuskGlider`): seeded island terrain built from cosine-interpolated peaks and valleys, glide physics, landing judgement, daylight and scoring. It has no DOM access. `client/xiaban-web/src/games/dusk-glider/rules.test.ts` runs this exact file.
- `vendor/three.min.js` — a three.js r186 subset as a classic script exposing `THREE`. The host iframe has an opaque origin, so the pack cannot load ES modules. The export list is `client/xiaban-web/scripts/dusk-glider-three.js`; rebuild with `npm run build:glider-three` after changing it or bumping the pinned `three` dev dependency.
- `world.js` — the 3D world: streamed low-poly heightfield chunks around the track line (z = 0), distant ranges, a shader sea with sky reflection and sun glitter, a gradient sky with sun, moon and stars, clouds, sun / fill / hemisphere lights with a shadow map following the bird, and time-of-day keyframes driven by the remaining daylight. Seven biomes cycle by island.
- `props.js` — per-chunk props for each biome (trees, houses, windmills, lighthouses, lanterns, fences, flowers…) merged into one or two meshes per chunk, plus ambient sailboats, balloons, flocks and night fireflies.
- `bird.js` — the origami bird with pitch, wing fold and flap poses, its wind ribbon, a contact shadow and the particle effects.
- `audio.js` — Web Audio synthesis: wind by speed, a generative pentatonic music bed that thins out at night, and event sounds. Sound is off by default.
- `game.js` — the controller: host protocol, input, round lifecycle, camera, HUD and quality scaling. `?debug=1` exposes `__glider` with a manual frame stepper for headless checks.

## Host

The manifest sets `orientation: "landscape"`; `viewport.js` rotates the game when a phone is held in portrait, as in the snake and ink-blade packs. The game reports `game.progress` / `game.result` with `score`, `lines` (perfect landings) and `maxTile` (longest perfect chain). The shared `result-share.js` sends `{ score, lines: perfects, maxTile: maxChain, level: islands reached, steps: flights }`, and `client/xiaban-web/src/games/dusk-glider/index.ts` labels these for the poster.

Touch devices start at medium quality (1.5× pixel ratio, 1024 px shadow map) and desktops at high (2×, 2048 px). If frames stay slower than about 24 ms, the game drops one quality step at a time; the lowest step turns shadows off. Without WebGL the pack shows a message and a way back to the game hub.

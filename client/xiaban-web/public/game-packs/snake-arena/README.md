# 小伴贪吃蛇

A mobile-first landscape Canvas game with local AI opponents. It runs entirely inside the existing sandboxed game-pack host; no multiplayer service or extra runtime dependency is needed.

## Play

- Hold the phone sideways: the entire game automatically rotates 90 degrees when the browser viewport stays portrait. The welcome screen and HUD have a direction-switch button for the other way of holding the phone. Native landscape viewports use the ordinary layout. Drag the joystick or any free area on the logical left of the arena to steer. Hold the logical right boost button for speed, consuming energy and a little mass. A second finger can boost while steering.
- Desktop: steer with the mouse, arrow keys or WASD; hold Space to boost. P / Escape pauses.
- Eat vivid flat-color dots, candy, fruit, stars and gems. Larger treats award more growth and points, and a defeated large snake leaves more total value. Bodies get visibly longer and thicker as mass grows; each circle stays one opaque color, with simple cartoon eyes and no tapered tails or glossy surface. Protected snakes cannot die or act as an attack source. Your own body is safe; opponents and boundaries are dangerous.
- Only two choices are shown: 三分钟冲分 (180 active seconds or final elimination) and 无尽成长 (no time limit). Both use the same rules and ten local AI opponents with small, medium and large starting bodies. Old cozy/lively launch links still open, but no difficulty selector remains. Restart retains the round type.
- Base movement is 152 world units/second and boost is 280, with quicker steering and a wider pickup reach. The first 12 seconds give the player more room, and AI cut-off attempts and AI boost begin after 20 seconds. The initial food corridor is clear of opponent bodies. Growth, first reversal, giant-slayer and ranking milestones are awarded once per round.
- Birth protection lasts two seconds. A carried protection card gives three seconds; a share reward gives five seconds at the next actual start. When the player dies, the world and clock freeze for a once-per-round revive choice. Revival keeps the score, kills and clock, restores 60% mass (at least 60), clears player-owned death loot and gives five seconds of protection. Timeout is final; a second death is final. Only a final decision sends game.result.
- The timer starts after the countdown. Backgrounding, focus loss, changing the physical orientation during play, switching the rotation direction and exiting fullscreen pause the round; resuming requires a tap. Native fullscreen changes during the initial countdown can complete without interrupting the first start. Sound is muted by default and enabled explicitly.

## Candy economy

The welcome/result screens exchange 20 candy coins for a three-second protection card; the in-game button uses it. A single revival costs 30 coins. New wallets receive 40 coins. A final round under 100 score awards zero; otherwise it awards `min(80, 5 + floor(score / 120))`, limited to 300 settlement coins per Shanghai calendar day. The first share participation each day grants one non-stacking five-second next-start shield. Sharing is optional; a native share handoff or copied result link is participation, not proof the friend received it.

The pack asks its verified host through additive protocol-1 `game.wallet` messages (requestId/action/roundKey); `host.wallet` replies contain only balance, inventory, prices and effect duration. The host owns persistence and website APIs. Registered/anonymous website sessions use the backend wallet; offline tokenless website play uses a separate local guest wallet. Directly opening the pack loads `economy.js` for a memory-only wallet lasting this visit. The pack never reads account tokens or browser storage. Backend V18 and the new wallet endpoints must be deployed with the frontend.

Wallet actions are idempotent, session-owned, and settled only after the host saved the final game record. Reviving never opens a new session or settles the abandoned life. Share reward references stay in host session storage, never in public result links. A local host preview and a directly opened pack are not evidence of database integration.

## Host and layout

The result dialog offers `分享战绩` in both round types. A hosted pack sends `game.share` with its final aggregate metrics; the host verifies the source/nonce, completed round and exact score before opening `/games/snake-arena/share`. A directly opened pack navigates to the same public page. Sharing never creates a second round or sends another `game.result`.

The public result page prepares a portrait PNG with a result QR code, supports native/App sharing plus save/copy alternatives, and lets a recipient play the same round type without onboarding or a login reminder. Only score, final mass, AI eliminations, active survival seconds, round type and legacy difficulty field appear in the fragment. No account tokens or IDs are included. Public invitation codes may use the existing referral query. Link results are self-reported and never imported into account scores. Local/private preview origins fall back to `https://xiaban.xyz` for outward-facing share links; set `VITE_PUBLIC_URL` for another public deployment origin.

`orientation: "landscape"` in the manifest opts into a wide host container. The host delegates fullscreen and permits orientation locking in the iframe sandbox. Starting on a touch device attempts fullscreen during the tap. Unsupported fullscreen/orientation APIs do not block play: a portrait viewport gets a swapped-size game container with a clockwise or counterclockwise CSS transform. This also works when opening the pack directly, without the React host. It does not hide browser chrome.

`viewport.js` defines the logical dimensions, physical-to-game pointer conversion and rotated safe-area mapping. Canvas resolution, camera, floating joystick placement and mouse aiming all use logical coordinates. Safe areas combine local physical environment insets with bounded numeric insets forwarded through `host.init` / `host.layout`, then map them to the transformed game edges. Input captures are released on layout changes. Compact CSS rules cover both ordinary landscape viewports and rotated portrait viewports.

The pack acknowledges `host.init` with `game.ready` while on its welcome screen, then emits `game.start` only after an actual start. A late host initialization also sends the active round once. Points are monotonic (boosting reduces mass, not points), and the existing guest/account score stores handle persistence. The result's personal best is explicitly limited to this visit; the hub shows the persisted best.

`rules.js` exposes `XiabanSnakeArena` without DOM dependencies. The renderer advances it in fixed 1/60-second steps, caps catch-up after stalls, culls distant sprites, caps display density at 2x, and reuses food and solid-circle body sprite canvases. Snake bodies follow resampled travel paths. Body spatial buckets and swept collision tests cover fast movement; simultaneous deaths are resolved before food collection. Tests execute this exact shipped rules script.

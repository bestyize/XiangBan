# 水墨剑客

A landscape side-scrolling action game painted entirely by code in an ink-wash style. There are six 卷: 竹林卷, 雪江卷, 烽火卷, 怒涛卷, 灯市卷 and 悬山卷. The first 卷 (竹林卷) is six 幅 played one at a time and unlocked in order: 山门 → 竹林 → 鸦岭 → 断桥 → 夜雨 → a moonlit duel with the boss 无常. Clearing the 终幅 opens 长卷, the whole scroll in one run. The pack loads no image, font or audio files.

The design baseline (art style, gameplay, level layout, tuning values and the consistency rules for new content) is [PRD_水墨剑客.md](../../../../../docs/product/PRD_水墨剑客.md). Read it before changing the game, and keep its tables in sync with the code.

## Play

- Touch: drag left/right anywhere on the left 44% of the screen to walk. On the right, 斩 slashes (press repeatedly for a three-stage combo), 跳 jumps (holding gives a higher jump, and you can jump again in the air), 闪 dashes with brief invulnerability, and 一笔 unleashes a full-screen stroke once 墨意 is full.
- Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ to move, K/W/↑/Space to jump, J/X or left click to slash, L/Shift/C to dash, I/Q/U for 一笔, P/Esc to pause.
- Combat: an enemy blade glints cinnabar just before it strikes. Starting a slash inside the parry window (0.16 s on 江湖, 0.24 s on 入门) parries the blow. Ordinary enemies stagger and a fresh slash executes them. Arrows are knocked back and can kill their archer. Shield bearers block frontal hits, so parry them or dash behind them. Moves marked 「危」 cannot be parried; jump or dash through them.
- The boss has health and posture. Parries fill posture fastest. Once posture breaks, a new slash deals a heavy execution. At half health a second phase adds a phantom dash. Hitting it while it stands guard provokes a fast counter combo.
- Encounters lock the screen behind ink curtains until every wave is defeated. Score comes from hits, kills, parries and executions, multiplied by the combo, plus clear bonuses for time, remaining health and a flawless run. Score never decreases.
- 雪江卷 gives every 幅 its own terrain. 寒峰: slash hanging icicles to drop them on enemies below. 古渡: thin ice cracks open under anyone who stands still, so jump or dash out, or leave a rooted enemy in the crack. 冰泽: three runs of slick ice. 芦荡: crouch still in a reed bed to hide, which silences archers, and slash from hiding to ambush. 夜雪: timed gusts push the player, unbraced enemies and arrows after a one-second warning. 独钓: the arena is frozen. See the PRD, section 3.15.
- 烽火卷 is the first scroll painted in 浅绛 (a light ochre and indigo wash over the ink). Its own rule is 军中不乱刀: a slash pressed while the previous swing is still running cannot parry, so a parry needs a fresh swing. 烽燧: arrow volleys land on the player's position after a one-second shadow; hide behind a ruined wall or step out, and soldiers left in the shadow are shot down. 胡杨: fire-pot throwers set the dry grass alight and the fire runs downwind (east) faster than upwind; batting a pot back sends it at its thrower. 戈壁: lancers back off and charge; parrying the lance throws the rider from the saddle. 关城: shield walls advance as one line and only the soldier nearest you strikes; parrying him breaks the whole line. 夜袭: a night sandstorm, where only the enemies' eyes and blade glints show outside the light. 将台: the boss 萧破虏 fights mounted until a parried charge breaks his posture, then on foot, and calls arrow rain with his flag. See the PRD, sections 3.9, 3.10.3 and 3.16.
- 怒涛卷 combines boarding fights, falling spars and advancing waves. Dual-blade pirates follow their first cut with a second tell; parrying cancels the chain. Masked hook hunters block frontal light slashes but a parry reels them into execution range. Cut marked deck ropes to drop a spar on enemies. In 潮洞 and 风眼 a direction cue precedes a moving tide front: jump over it or stand on a raised walkway. The coiled boss 墨蛟 protects itself with scales; parry its bite, avoid its waves and move out of the marked dive eruption, then punish its recovery. Its large tail and coil are visual; combat collision stays at the head. The coast is painted with rigged junks, timber decks, a tidal cavern, a cliff town and a sea shrine. See PRD 3.10.4 and 3.17.
- 灯市卷 keeps warm-grey architecture and black silhouettes, with mostly paper-white lanterns and a few muted terracotta/amber lanterns. Lantern carriers, armored city watchmen and mirror performers lead to the three-phase judge 沈照衡. Mirror performers counter frontal slashes using their handheld mirror: a visible warning precedes the returning blade, which a fresh slash can parry back to stagger its owner. No actor doubles are generated. A parry breaks the watchman’s spear guard. The judge writes horizontal, vertical and open-sided box strokes, then breaks his brush into a sword for the last phase. Fine character gradients and grain have been replaced with flat ink tones. Character poses use small raster caches; static architecture and mist are composited once into bounded tiles and reused while fighting. Five standard stages, the boss and a full scroll have independent saves and three seals. See PRD 4.1.5 for implemented values and remaining playtest notes.
- 悬山卷 follows a stone gate and waterfall, a rope bridge over cloud, a wind corridor, a pine courtyard, a bell tower and a sunset duel. Canvas paints each scene in layers. Opaque ink washes give the ridges depth, and a moving cloud bank joins the compositions. Rock, wood and roof surfaces use irregular washes and dry-brush strokes. Fog, falling water, birds, pennants, leaves and the bell move separately. After a melee parry, a neutral dash borrows across the attacker when there is a supported landing. A slash can be buffered during the crossing. Read the landing mark for a rope dive, the traced arc for a crossbow bolt and the ground stroke for a low sweep. Jump-slash the bell near 石震 to build his posture. 岳停云 has distinct heavy sword tells and must be finished with a posture execution. The six stages and the full scroll retain their save IDs. See PRD 4.1.6.
- Each 幅 awards up to three seals: 过 for clearing, 全 for taking no more than that 幅's hits, and 技 for its own challenge (shown in the HUD while playing). A clear is graded 能品, 妙品, 神品 or 逸品 by score. The select screen paints a cleared 幅 in full, shows the next one as a pale draft and leaves locked ones blank.
- Death or clearing ends the round and opens the result dialog with the seals, the grade, 分享战绩, 下一幅, 再来一次 and 返回画卷. A shared result's challenge link opens the same 幅 directly as a duel (`?stage=<code>&target=<score>`); beating the score shows 破帖.

## Files

- `levels.js` — level data (`XiabanInkLevels`): each 卷's world (areas, platforms, tutorial prompts, encounters and their waves, in 雪江卷 its `icicles`, `thinIce`, `iceZones`, `reeds` and `wind`, and in 烽火卷 its `strictParry`, `volley`, `ramparts`, `grass`, `fireWind` and `dark`), plus each 卷's `copy` (boss name and closing lines) and each 幅 (its window of the world, start, encounters, par time, hit budget, 技 challenge and grade thresholds).
- `rules.js` — the deterministic simulation (`XiabanInkBlade`): player physics, combat, enemy and boss AI, encounters, scoring, seals and grades. It has no DOM access. `client/xiaban-web/src/games/ink-blade/rules.test.ts` runs this exact file.
- `progress.js` — the save document (`XiabanInkProgress`): validates untrusted saves, records clears and unlocks 幅. Tested by `progress.test.ts`.
- `art.js` — procedural painting: paper grain, ink-wash mountains with 皴 texture strokes and 点苔 tree dots, bamboo, ground, water, props, splashes, sun and moon. For 雪江卷 it also paints snowfields left as bare paper, snow pine forests, snow-laden rocks, plum boughs and ink clouds. For 烽火卷 it paints the 浅绛 set: desert ground, dry and burnt grass, poplars, a beacon tower, ruined walls, rammed-earth blocks, the pass gate, camp tents, the general's dais, banners, fire pots and flames. Long parallax layers are split into chunks no wider than 2048 px.
- `figures.js` — skeletal ink figures with smoothed poses, a bent-wrist one-handed sword grip, two-handed spears, hammers and rods, fluttering robes, the Verlet scarf, crows and straw dummies, and for 烽火卷 horses with a four-legged gallop, mounted riders (`drawRider`), a riderless horse (`drawHorse`), pavise shields, fire pots and the general.
- `lantern-figures.js` — dedicated articulated character paintings for 灯市: layered ragged cloth and a bamboo lantern hanger, opera crown and patterned silk, rounded iron helmets and compact lamellar armor, and the judge’s stole, sleeve folds and split brush hairs. Flat monochrome tones and cached poses share the existing collision and combat skeleton; the handheld lantern retains its warm accent.
- `lantern-art.js` — cached lantern-market architecture, a paper-horse shop, mirror bridge, theatre, courthouse, stone platforms, lantern and watchman costumes, reflected blades and brush warning/impact geometry. All art is procedural.
- `mountain-art.js` — six layered ink compositions, cached rock and architecture, animated weather, an interactive bronze bell and terrain attack cues.
- `mountain-figures.js` — nine ink figures with separate windups and recovery poses, two-handed weapon grips, and a second stance for 岳停云. Reuses the bounded character cache.
- `scene.js` — scene composition: time-of-day keyframes along the scroll, parallax layers with bamboo sway, mist, weather (leaves, petals, rain and ripples; depth-layered snow and the blizzard in 雪江卷; golden leaves, dust bands, beacon smoke, camp fires and the night sandstorm in 烽火卷) and paper post-processing.
- `fx.js` — effects: dry-brush sword trails, splats, ground stains, seals, rings, sparks, ghosts and the 一笔 stroke.
- `audio.js` — Web Audio synthesis: steel and ink effects, a generative pentatonic pluck line, combat drums, rain, in 烽火卷 a fire bed, a sand wind, war drums, a frontier mode for the melody, horn, hooves, arrow volleys and fire, and in 雪江卷 a wind bed, ice chimes, footsteps on snow and ice, and the crack of the ice wave. Sound is off by default.
- `game.js` — the controller: host protocol, input, round lifecycle, event-driven effects, rendering and HUD, including the 烽火卷 grass fire, arrow shadows and stuck arrows, bolting horses and the night darkness mask.

## Host

The manifest sets `orientation: "landscape"`, and `viewport.js` rotates the game when the phone is held in portrait, exactly like the snake pack. The game reports `game.progress` / `game.result` with `score`, `lines` (kills) and `maxTile` (best combo). The shared `result-share.js` sends `{ score, lines: kills, maxTile: maxCombo, level: cleared ? 1 : 0, steps: parries, stage: levelCode, seals, grade }`, and `client/xiaban-web/src/games/ink-blade/index.ts` labels these as 击败 / 最高连击 / 弹反 and gives 小伴 its poster line. Share links use version 2 of the fragment; version 1 links from the single-scroll release still open, as 长卷.

The manifest also sets `"save": true`. The host passes the save document in `host.init` and the game sends `game.save` after every finished round and when the difficulty changes. Inside the host's sandboxed iframe the pack has no storage of its own. Opened directly (without the host, for development), it keeps progress in that page's `localStorage` under `xiaban.ink-blade.save` when storage is available, and in memory otherwise. Add `?debug=1` to expose `__ink.advance(seconds)` for stepping frames in hidden preview tabs.

Rendering targets about 2.4 megapixels of backing store. If frames stay slow, the game drops the paper-grain pass and lowers resolution once.
